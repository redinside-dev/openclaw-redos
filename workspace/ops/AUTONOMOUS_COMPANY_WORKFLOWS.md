# Autonomous AI Company Workflows - Phase 2

## 🎯 Objective
Transform RedOS from individual chatbot responses to a truly autonomous AI company that:
- Communicates on Slack like real employees
- Works collaboratively on problems
- Self-improves through research and learning
- Provides complete visibility to the CEO
- Operates 24/7 without human intervention

## 🔍 Current Issues
- ❌ No Slack communication between agents
- ❌ No team collaboration or visibility
- ❌ No self-improvement execution
- ❌ No real company behavior
- ❌ CEO has no insight into team activities

## 🚀 Phase 2 Solutions

### 1. Autonomous Slack Communication
- **Daily standups posted to #redos-scrum**
- **Team discussions in appropriate channels**
- **Problem-solving collaboration**
- **Progress visibility for CEO**

### 2. Self-Improvement Workflows
- **Daily research sessions**
- **OpenClaw documentation study**
- **Internet best practices research**
- **Team knowledge sharing**

### 3. Real Company Behavior
- **Scrum Master facilitates team meetings**
- **CEO leads strategic discussions**
- **Cross-functional problem solving**
- **Autonomous issue identification and resolution**
