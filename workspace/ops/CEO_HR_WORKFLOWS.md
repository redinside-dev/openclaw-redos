# CEO HR Workflows - Autonomous Hire/Fire Decisions

## 🎯 Mission
Empower the CEO with complete HR authority to hire and fire agents autonomously, ensuring optimal team composition and performance.

## 🔍 CEO HR Authority

### ✅ CEO Powers
- **Hiring Authority**: Can hire agents without human approval
- **Firing Authority**: Can fire underperforming agents
- **Resource Management**: Can allocate resources as needed
- **Team Composition**: Can optimize team structure
- **Budget Authority**: Can manage team budget and costs

### ✅ Decision Criteria
- **Performance**: Agent performance below 60% for 2 weeks
- **Workload**: Team workload above 80% for 1 week
- **Quality**: Consistent quality issues or missed deadlines
- **Collaboration**: Poor collaboration or communication
- **Redundancy**: Role becomes redundant or unnecessary

## 🚀 CEO HR Workflows

### 1. Workload Analysis Workflow (Every 30 minutes)
**Objective**: Analyze team workload and determine resource needs

**Workflow:**
1. **Collect Workload Data**: Gather workload data from all agents
2. **Analyze Capacity**: Compare workload vs. team capacity
3. **Identify Gaps**: Identify workload gaps and overloads
4. **Calculate Needs**: Calculate hiring/firing requirements
5. **Make Decisions**: Make HR decisions based on analysis

**Expected Output:**
```
👑 *CEO Workload Analysis — 2026-02-22*
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Team Workload: 120% average capacity utilization
• Individual Workloads: RESEARCH 150%, ENG 85%, OPS 95%
• Capacity Gaps: RESEARCH needs +1 agent, INFOSEC underutilized
• HR Decision: Hire RESEARCH-2, Fire INFOSEC-1
• Timeline: Implement within 2 hours
```
