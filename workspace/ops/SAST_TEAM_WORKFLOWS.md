# SAST Team Workflows - Autonomous Research & Analysis

## 🎯 Mission
The SAST team (RESEARCH) is responsible for proactive research, internet scanning, and knowledge discovery to continuously improve the AI company's capabilities and performance.

### ❌ Current (Idle)
- No proactive research happening
- No internet scanning for best practices
- No knowledge sharing with team
- No delegation of research tasks
- No trend analysis or competitor analysis

### ✅ Intended (Autonomous)
- **Daily Research Cycle**: 6+ research findings per day
- **Internet Scanning**: Continuous monitoring of AI/ML trends
- **Knowledge Sharing**: Research findings shared across team
- **Delegation**: Research tasks delegated to appropriate specialists

- **Trend Analysis**: Industry trends and competitor analysis

## 🚀 Autonomous Workflows

### 1. Daily Research Cycle (Every 4 hours)
**Objective**: Proactive research and knowledge discovery

**Workflow:**
1. **Internet Research**: Scan for AI/ML best practices, OpenClaw updates
2. **Knowledge Discovery**: Find actionable insights for team improvement
3. **Documentation**: Create knowledge base entries and learning resources
4. **Delegation**: Research tasks delegated to appropriate specialists
5. **Sharing**: Post findings to #openclaw-optimization for team visibility

**Expected Output:**
```

```
