### 2. **Contamination-Aware Evaluation**
**Pattern:** Benchmark contamination (training data leakage) is a growing concern

**Evidence:**
- RefineBench shows frontier models have low baseline in self-refinement
- But with targeted checklist feedback, models can refine to near-perfect within ~5 turns

**Implementation for RedOS:**
1. **Private/Rotated Held-Out Sets** - Never publish with model providers
2. **Contamination-Adjusted pass@1** - Exclude tasks in public benchmarks
3. **Bootstrap CI Lower-Bound Gating** - Require 95% CI lower bound > 0
4. **Memorization Probes** - Include "canary" tasks with known solutions

**Impact:** MEDIUM - Evaluation quality
**OpenClaw Owner:** RESEARCH + ENG for Skill Optimizer
**Effort:** 1-2 days

### 3. **Iterative Retrieval Pattern**
**Pattern:** 4-phase loop (DISPATCH → EVALUATE → REFINE → LOOP) solves subagent context problem

**Benefits:**
- Learns codebase terminology in first cycle

- Maximum 3 cycles prevents infinite loops

**Impact:** MEDIUM - Context management efficiency
**OpenClaw Owner:** ENG for subagent coordination
**Effort:** 2 days
