## 2) Market regime checklist (quick diagnostics)
Use this as the weekly “are we in risk-on or risk-off?” scoreboard:

### A) Rates / macro
- **10Y yields:** rising quickly → pressure on long-duration growth; falling → supports growth multiples.
- **Real rates & inflation expectations:** if real rates push up, market typically de-rates.
- **Fed posture:** watch for language that changes the implied path (more important than any single datapoint).

### B) Breadth / concentration
- **Equal-weight vs cap-weight:** if equal-weight outperforms, it’s a breadth-friendly tape.
- **Advance/decline behavior:** weak breadth + strong index often = fragile.

### C) Volatility / risk appetite
- **Volatility pickup:** usually rotates leadership to defensives.
- **Credit stress:** widening spreads = risk-off signal.

## 3) Current narratives to track (what headlines are really about)
### 3.1 Rotation narrative
- **Story:** money rotates from expensive, crowded trades toward **value/small-caps/industrials/energy/materials**.
- **Why it can persist:** investors start demanding **earnings breadth** and better “value per dollar” of growth.
- **How it fails:** sudden rate drop + renewed AI/tech momentum; or macro deterioration that punishes cyclicals.

### 3.2 Earnings narrative (micro dominates)
- **Story:** guidance, margins, and cost of capital decide winners.
- **What to listen for:** demand softness, pricing power, wage pressure, inventory levels, and buyback posture.

### 3.3 Policy / geopolitics / tariffs (tail risk)
- **Story:** policy shocks can reprice supply chains and energy quickly.
- **Conservative posture:** don’t bet the book on tail outcomes; instead, build resilience (diversification, liquidity, position sizing).

## 4) Sector playbook (conservative, practical)

### Defensives (staples / healthcare)
- **When they work:** volatility up, growth scare, or “risk-off”.
- **What to monitor:** pricing power, reimbursement/regulatory chatter (healthcare), input costs.

### Financials
- **When they work:** stable-to-higher rates with benign credit; improving breadth.
- **Key risks:** credit deterioration, deposit competition, regulatory changes.
- **Monitor:** NIM trend, charge-offs, loan growth, management tone.

### Industrials
- **When they work:** cyclical optimism, capex cycle, infrastructure-like themes.
- **Key risks:** PMI slowdown, order cancellations, margin compression.
- **Monitor:** backlog quality, lead indicators (PMI, freight), pricing power.

### Energy
- **When they work:** crude stability/uptrend, supply discipline.
- **Key risks:** crude drawdown, policy shocks, demand shock.
- **Monitor:** crude curve, inventories, OPEC+ headlines.

### Tech / “AI infrastructure”
- **When it works:** rates stable/down, strong earnings visibility.
- **Key risks:** crowded positioning, valuation, capex cycle disappointment.
- **Monitor:** guidance on spend, unit economics, customer concentration.
